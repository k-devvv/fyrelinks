## 1. Regulatory Context & Scope
* **Regulation**: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act).
* **Core Distinction**: **Providers** (who train and market AI models) versus **Deployers** (who integrate AI systems in user-facing applications).
* **Risk Categorization**:
  * Unacceptable Risk: Prohibited (social scoring, subliminal manipulation).
  * High Risk: Strict conformity assessment, human oversight, logging, risk management (employment, critical infrastructure, law enforcement).
  * General-Purpose AI (GPAI): Transparency obligations, copyright policy compliance, model documentation.
  * Minimal/Low Risk: Transparency notice required when users interact with an AI system (e.g. customer support bots).

## 2. Key Developer Obligations for Standard LLM Applications
1. **Transparency**: Inform users clearly that they are interacting with an AI system unless obvious from context.
2. **Synthetic Media Labeling**: Ensure outputs that constitute deepfakes or synthetic video/audio are marked in a machine-readable format.
3. **Technical Documentation**: Maintain records of training datasets, architecture, and testing procedures if developing high-risk systems.
