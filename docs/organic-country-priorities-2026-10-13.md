## Measurement protocol
Record the eventual production release date separately from the commit date. Compare 28 complete days before versus 28 after, and use seven-day views only as early directional checks. Exclude incomplete recent days.
- Segment US, UK, Canada, Germany, India and Brazil separately.
- Track query + landing page + device, clicks, impressions, CTR and position together.
- Compare the same query/page combinations; changing query mix can move aggregate CTR without a snippet effect.
- Treat gains as observations, not proof of causation. At present volume, one click changes the rate materially.
- Track consented engaged sessions and useful directory/source interactions separately from search impressions.
- Keep one primary URL per intent; do not produce thin country copies, geo-keyword lists or fabricated local addresses.
- Use hreflang only for real, accessible equivalent translations/region variants. No IP redirects or fake geolocation meta tags.
- Community distribution requires a helpful substantive contribution, disclosure of ownership and compliance with each community's rules. No outreach or promotional posts were sent in this work.
